# Osaka-jo Hall

## Reference frame and exterior

The local origin is at 34.68965545° N, 135.53009923° E. Positive X follows the long wall toward the southeast. Positive Z points southwest. The north lobby is on the negative-Z side between seating blocks G and H.

The footprint and measured height of 20.9 m are from building `bldg_76be51df-af78-4437-b4f4-aac6dff0dd62` in the 2022 Osaka City PLATEAU CityGML dataset. The model uses the surveyed perimeter and a reconstructed intermediate roof profile. The surrounding buildings use simplified footprint rings and published heights in the same frame. Their wall finishes are illustrative. [MLIT Project PLATEAU, Osaka City 2022](https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/plateau-27100-osaka-shi-2022)

Default ground colours follow the 2022 PLATEAU land-use and road polygons. The two sports fields and forecourt are traced from the local orthophoto. The viewer repeats paving, grass and asphalt grain in metres and uses approximate planting positions sampled within park areas. The aerial ground switch replaces only the outside surface and is stored in shared links as `aerial=1`.

The optional ground image and roof finish are local, rotated crops of MLIT PLATEAU-Ortho 2024. The ground covers 960 m across. The roof crop covers 140 m along the long axis. The fountain plaza, diagonal north approach and riverside terraces were positioned by comparing the aerial view with the operator's arrival photographs. [PLATEAU-Ortho tiles](https://tile.plateauview.mlit.go.jp/tiles/plateau-ortho-2024/17/114883/52053.png) · [Official north entrance approach](https://www.osaka-johall.com/access/osakajo-koen/)

## Public interior

The visitor model follows the operator's 1F arena and 2F stand diagrams, six public arena gates and east/west stair connections. Concessions use the operator's published pillar references, 08 and 14 on 1F and 14 on 2F. Walking widths, stair flights, floor elevations, individual signs and finishes are reconstructed. Gate animations illustrate an interaction in the viewer. [Official floor guide](https://www.osaka-johall.com/floorguide/) · [Concession locations](https://www.osaka-johall.com/floorguide/shop/)

Stand seat numbers follow the public seat lookup. The three stage presets follow the venue's A, B and C layouts. Movable seats, screens and concert lighting represent sample productions. The ceiling includes a central sound cluster and 18 distributed speakers. [Official seat plans](https://www.osaka-johall.com/seat/) · [Venue equipment](https://www.osaka-johall.com/organizer/arena/)

The north terrace stonework, orange stand seats and locker finishes use the operator's visitor photos as visual references. The small gate directories are original Stage EX graphics. Door hardware, lighting fixtures and the speaker arrays on the sample stage are illustrative. [North entrance photographs](https://www.osaka-johall.com/access/osakajo-koen/) · [Public facility photos](https://www.osaka-johall.com/floorguide/)

The schedule reads music performances listed for the arena in the operator’s monthly calendar and filters by the current date in Japan. The daily Pages build refreshes the snapshot. [Official event calendar](https://www.osaka-johall.com/event/)
