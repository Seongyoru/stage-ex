# K-Arena Yokohama (Kアリーナ横浜) - Interior Spaces and Facilities

Research angle: everything inside the building except seat counting, with heavy visual analysis.
Research date: 2026-11-23. About 95 photographs / drawings were opened and studied visually (log in section 13).

Confidence tags used on every fact:

- **CONFIRMED** - stated by an official / primary source (operator site, Ken Corporation release, Sony, L-Acoustics, Kotobuki Seating) or directly readable on an official drawing / sign.
- **REPORTED** - credible secondary source (press, trade press, fan blog).
- **ESTIMATED** - derived by me; the derivation is given. Treat as a modelling default, not as survey data.

Colour hex values are always ESTIMATED (sampled by eye from photographs taken under mixed lighting).

Source keys (full URLs in section 15): OFF-FLOOR, OFF-SEAT, OFF-SHOP, OFF-FAQ, KEN-PR, SONY, LA, BESTEC, KOTO, TW, IW, AVW, ORI1, ORI0, MP, RS, SK, WIKI, MONTA, HNMRK, HIRANO, IDEAL, BEESPITZ, KENBIYA, DIME, LTIKE, TJ.

## 3. The hall (bowl)

### 3.1 Plan shape

- End-stage hall, fan shaped; every stand seat faces the stage (CONFIRMED, operator wording "全席が音の正面").
- The plan is faceted, not curved: a straight centre section plus two angled wing facets per side on each tier (CONFIRMED visually from the official seat map and from the stage-view photo).
- Side walls converge toward the stage: the hall is narrowest at the stage end and widest about half-way down the arena (CONFIRMED visually in the top-view photo).
- Arena chair layout: 7 blocks across x 2 blocks deep; the two outer blocks are trapezoids; 6 longitudinal aisles; seat numbers 1-140 across (20 per block) (CONFIRMED, OFF-SEAT map; block map also photographed on a show day by HNMRK).

### 3.2 Stage-end and side walls - the signature look

This is the most recognisable interior feature and must be modelled.

- The stage end and both side walls (as far back as the ends of the stands) are wrapped by **three stacked technical galleries** with open steel-pipe handrails (3 horizontal rails) (CONFIRMED visually: Kotobuki, Oricon official, L-Acoustics photos).
- Each gallery tier is backed by a **continuous floor-to-soffit pleated curtain** in red-violet / wine velour. Travel Watch identifies these as the sound-absorbing material ("カーテン状のものが吸音材") (REPORTED). A fourth, lower band of the same curtain hangs at arena level behind the stage (about the centre 55 % of the width).
- Each band is lit from its head by a continuous linear cove, cool white in the official photographs (curtain reads magenta-pink, about #B45CA8) and warm in press photos (curtain reads copper / terracotta, about #B5654F). Unlit, the fabric is a deep wine (about #7D2F5E).
- The curtains are **operable**: official photos show them both fully closed and gathered into bunches about every 4 m, tied back diagonally, exposing a dark olive-grey panelled wall (about #5E5A4E) with the cove light still on (CONFIRMED visually, Oricon official top view). Model them as a variable-absorption element with two states.
- Gallery structure: dark grey painted H-section steel columns and beams with bolted splice plates; gallery floors pale beige concrete (CONFIRMED visually, L-Acoustics close-up).
- At the sides, the fascia of the lowest gallery is a black band carrying a row of about 10-12 round ports per side, about 0.5 m diameter (CONFIRMED visually; function not stated - ESTIMATED to be long-throw air-supply jet nozzles).
- Above the top curtain tier the wall is plain charcoal (about #26272B) with a soft scalloped wall-wash, up to the roof steel.
- Beside the stage, left and right, stand black steel 3-level technical towers with round posts and grating floors, carrying moving lights (CONFIRMED visually, Travel Watch 021 / 022, Oricon official).
- House side walls behind the stands: dark charcoal, vertically ribbed / perforated panels (CONFIRMED visually; exact product unknown).

### 3.3 Ceiling

- Fully exposed, everything matte black: deep steel roof trusses (about 7 m deep, ESTIMATED), secondary steel, catwalks, cable trays (CONFIRMED visually).
- A regular grid of round high-bay downlights is fixed in the truss zone; white in cleaning mode (ESTIMATE about 12 x 10 fittings visible).
- A large **black steel gallery bridge** (the "pin-spot gallery" ピンスポットギャラリー of the L-Acoustics text) spans the hall in front of the Upper Stand, U-shaped in plan, two rail levels, mesh / solid black infill. It carries the 8 follow spots, 14 delay loudspeaker clusters (6 x 4 K2 + 8 x 4 K3) and, hanging beneath its stage-side edge, the 4 upper service screens (CONFIRMED: BESTEC text + photos).
- A second, smaller bridge stack sits on the centreline above the stage front (lighting / elevating grids) (CONFIRMED visually).
- Hanging absorbers about 0.9 x 1.8 m are suspended in large numbers in the maintenance deck (REPORTED, BESTEC); they are not individually visible in photographs because everything is black. ESTIMATE for modelling: vertical black baffles on a 2 m grid above the catwalk level.
- Dozens of chain-hoist lines hang permanently over the stage zone (CONFIRMED visually).

## 4. Entrance sequence and vertical circulation

1. Visitors arrive on the **Arena Deck** (LEVEL 3 outside) by the Big Steps from Music Terrace Square, by the North Gate stair, by the South Gate, or by the Takashima Suisaisen deck from Yokohama Station (opened 2025-03-27, about 130 m long, 6 m wide - REPORTED, MONTA / LTIKE).
2. Three entrances on LEVEL 3: **Main Entrance** on the diagonal face toward the Hilton, **North Entrance** and **South Entrance**; staff assign one per show. Wheelchair users are sent to the South Gate (elevator and slope) (CONFIRMED, OFF-FAQ, TW).
3. Ticket check at the doors, then the **Main Foyer** (アリーナメインホワイエ): a wide, low, dark hall dominated by the red grand stair.
4. From the foyer:
   - Lower Stand: same level, gates 301-302 to the left, 303-304 to the right (signs on the columns).
   - Arena: red side stairs down ("LEVEL 1 ARENA", diagonal arrow) to gates 101-104.
   - LEVEL 4: up the grand stair (about 23 risers) to the landing under the "K-Arena" logotype.
   - LEVEL 5: 2 escalators (left and right of the foyer, entered through a red portal wall with an arched cut-out) or stairs "3-4-5".
   - LEVEL 7: stairs "3-5-7" only, about 90 steps above LEVEL 5; elevators exist but are staff-controlled and absent from public maps (CONFIRMED / REPORTED).
5. LEVEL 1 and LEVEL 3 interconnect inside the bowl; LEVEL 5 and LEVEL 7 are reached only from the foyer (REPORTED, HNMRK).
6. After the show, exit is regulated block by block; full clearance takes 40-50 minutes (REPORTED, LTIKE).

Stairs on the official maps: 4 x "LEVEL 1-3" at the rear of the arena; 3 x "LEVEL 1-3-4-5" (centre grand stair + 2 flanking); 4 x "LEVEL 3-4-5" behind gates 505-508; about 6-8 x "LEVEL 3-5-7" spread along the wings and at the North / South Entrances (CONFIRMED, counted on OFF-FLOOR maps; exact number +-1).
