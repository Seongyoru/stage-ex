# K-Arena Yokohama (Kアリーナ横浜) — Exterior Form, Facade and Surrounding Site

Research angle: exterior massing, facade system, signage, night appearance, site and props.
Compiled 2026-11-23. About 50 photos / drawings were opened and studied visually (list in section 12).

Confidence tags: **CONFIRMED** = official / primary source states it. **REPORTED** = credible secondary source. **ESTIMATED** = derived by me (derivation given).

---

## 0. Quick orientation (read this first)

- The building is **not** aligned east-west. The fan (audience) end and main entrance face **north-west (bearing about 302 deg)** toward the JR freight line, Takashima Suisaisen Park (高島水際線公園) and the Katabira River mouth (帷子川河口). The stage end faces **south-east (about 123-125 deg)**.
- In plan the roof is an **irregular hexagon**: a narrow stage end (about 42 m), two long straight sides (about 126 m each) diverging at about 66 deg, and a three-facet "fan front" (about 59 + 77 + 63 m).
- Only the **three front facets** carry the signature element: a huge upward-tilted **eave (庇) projecting 9 m**, carried by a row of about 25 square exposed-concrete columns 1.2 m x 1.2 m x 30 m. The two long sides and the stage end are plain, sheer white panel walls with no eave.
- Hilton Yokohama (ヒルトン横浜) and K Tower Yokohama (Kタワー横浜) stand in a row directly **west** of the arena's south-west corner (order west to east: K Tower, Hilton, Arena). The raised plaza "Music Terrace" (ミュージックテラス) lies north of the towers and west / north-west of the arena front.
- Everything public happens on raised decks: plaza deck about +4.5 m, arena terrace (Level 3, main entrance) about +6 m above the street.

## 3. Massing, side by side

### 3.1 Main front — north-west facet (faces 302 deg; plaza side, also the rail / river side)

Seen from Yokohama Station direction, Minato Mirai Ohashi (みなとみらい大橋), the park and the deck. Photos T2, B1, B2, B3, K6, K7, A1.
- Silhouette: a broad, low "hat brim". A flat-looking roof with a slightly raised central roof plane, a thick eave slab tilted upward toward its outer lip, and a colonnade of tall thin concrete columns below it. The brim wraps three facets, so from the front you see the main facet frontally and the two side facets receding at about 33 deg each.
- Eave profile (section, ESTIMATED from T2, W2, section render): soffit starts at the top of the inclined upper wall, crosses the column heads at about +36 m, continues rising at about 20-25 deg for 9 m to the lip at about +41 m. The lip is a rounded nose (radius about 1-1.5 m). Above the lip a darker standing-seam roof skirt slopes back up to the main roof (+45 m). The eave ends at each side with a pointed "prow" where it meets the long side wall (photos K3, K4, W2).
- Columns: about **25 in total** (ESTIMATED: 23 counted in the frontal photo B2 plus 2-3 hidden behind Hilton): about 10 on the main facet at about 8.0 m centres (9 bays over a column line of about 72 m), about 7-8 on each side facet at about 7.5-8 m. Column line is 9 m inside the eave edge. Each column is a plain square prism 1.2 m, exposed fair-faced concrete, with a 0.5 m plinth.
- Wall zone behind the columns, bottom to top: (a) terrace-level concourse — dark doors and a two-tier curtain wall about 8 m high; (b) a white, horizontally ribbed upper wall that **leans outward** about 20 deg (it is the underside of the upper stand), almost touching the column line at its top, cut by black strip windows and by faceted triangular glazed corners (photo B11); (c) the soffit.
- **Glazed lobby box** (the "glazed lobby strip"): a rectangular glass volume centred on the main facet, about **46 m wide** (5.5-6 column bays; ESTIMATED from B1, B2), about **20 m tall** above the terrace, projecting from the wall to just in front of the column line so that 6 columns pass through / rise out of its roof. It has an open ground storey (entrance doors between concrete piers, about 4-4.5 m high) and **4 glazed tiers** above (levels 4-7: foyer, Lounge 5, VIP lounge, Arena Bar 7). The side returns are slightly chamfered.
- Main Entrance: 5-6 door bays under the glass box; each bay has 3 pairs of black-framed glass double doors with full-height stainless pull bars (photo W4).
- Roof sign "K-Arena" stands on the roof skirt above the centre of the glass box.

### 3.2 North facet (faces 337 deg) — North Entrance, goods area

Photos B3, K5, K6, T2 (left part), B7, B11.
- Same eave and colonnade (7-8 columns). No glass box: the wall shows the two-tier concourse glazing at terrace level (about 5 glazed bays between columns), then white ribbed wall with two rows of black slots.
- The eave stops at the N corner (P5) with a vertical, slightly raked end face; beyond it the NE long wall begins.
- On the terrace in front: the **Arena Tent** (sawtooth-roof goods-sales tent, about 45 m long).
- Below the terrace: a 2-storey white podium wall with fine vertical ribbing / stripes, a brick-red lift tower, the "North Gate" stairs and ramps down to the north road, bicycle parking and the P1 car park entrance at ground level.

### 3.3 West facet (faces 270 deg) — South Entrance, toward Hilton

Photos T5, T8, B9, W2, K8.
- Same eave and colonnade (7-8 columns); the eave lip comes within a few metres of the Hilton tower (a narrow slot of sky).
- A glazed **sky-bridge** with exposed red zig-zag (Warren) truss crosses from the arena (about one storey above the terrace, underside about 5 m above the deck) to the 3rd storey of the Hilton podium; length about 15-20 m, about 3 m wide, about 3.5 m high (ESTIMATED from T8, B9). Travel Watch: "ヒルトン横浜とは渡り廊下でも接続".
- The Level-3 terrace wraps round this corner and continues as a balcony along the south side.

### 3.4 South long side (faces 180 deg) — toward the vacant lot and Anpanman Museum

Photos T5, K3, K8, B10 (aerial).
- A single sheer wall about 126 m long of flat white rectangular panels. Its top edge **slopes down from the fan end (west, about +43 m) to the stage end (east, about +32 m)**, a slope of about 5 deg.
- West end: the wall runs out into a sharp prow under the eave lip. Below the prow a large **triangular wedge of horizontally ribbed siding** (the raking stand underside) fills the lower west third; its upper edge rises at about 30 deg from the podium band up to mid-height at the west end; it has 3-4 tiny black slots.
- East third: a **recessed rectangular opening about 10 m wide x 13 m high** containing two open service galleries with X-braced railings (mechanical balconies), dark inside. ESTIMATED from B6 (12.6 px/m).
- Base: a continuous horizontal band at about +6.5 to +8 m (service balcony / deck edge, light grey with a louvred balustrade), then a recessed dark-grey ground storey with service doors. A low white wall and young trees along the kerb.
- A darker standing-seam roof skirt (about 4-5 m high, mid-grey) is visible above the parapet from high viewpoints (B10).

### 3.5 North-east long side (faces 66 deg) — toward the road and block 62

Photos K1, K9, K10, B5, B6. Mirror image of the south side: sheer panel wall about 127 m, parapet sloping down toward the stage end, ribbed triangular wedge at the fan (north) end, the same two-gallery recessed opening about 40 m from the stage-end corner, a band of dark grey horizontal louvres / mesh about 3 m high under the panels, a white band, then a dark blue-grey ground storey behind concrete planter walls and street trees.

### 3.6 Stage end (faces about 125 deg, about 42 m wide)

Photos K2, B6 (left edge).
- The corners of the main panel volume are **rounded (radius about 3 m)**; the upper white volume overhangs the darker podium by about 2-3 m with a slotted soffit.
- A separate panel-clad block (stage house / backstage, about 20 m wide, slightly tilted faces, also round-cornered) stands off the main volume by a dark recess about 4 m wide.
- An external steel stair tower was under scaffold at the NE-side / stage-end junction in May 2023 (K9).
- **Loading docks:** not confirmed by any drawing. Press reports only say the load-in route is one-way (ワンウェイ方式) so trucks pass through without turning. ESTIMATE: truck entry in the dark ground storey on the NE-side road near the stage end, drive-through behind the stage at Level 1, exit on the south side; model two roller-shutter openings about 5 m wide x 4.5 m high on each long side within 30 m of the stage end.

### 3.7 Roof (fifth elevation)

Photos S1 (satellite), B10, B1.
- Hexagonal, very light grey metal standing-seam roof. A **ridge runs along the long axis** (bearing 303 deg) from the stage end to the middle of the main facet; the two planes fall gently toward the two long sides. Whole roof also falls from fan end (+45 m) to stage end (about +36 m).
- South plane: visible parallel ribs running perpendicular to the south edge. North-east plane: smoother, with a coarse grid of expansion joints (about 8 x 5 fields).
- Perimeter: on the three front facets a white rim about 3 m wide (top of the eave lip, with an inner gutter well) and inside it a darker grey sloping skirt 5-6 m wide; on the long sides a mid-grey sloping skirt about 4-5 m high between wall parapet and roof plane with a few long vent hoods.
- No rooftop plant visible on the main roof; small equipment along the south edge.

## 6. Night appearance

Sources: official night photo T2 (Kアリーナマネジメント), W1, W4, W5, render R1.
- **Eave:** the soffit is washed from uplights at the column heads. Default is warm-white / neutral white, giving a bright floating slab with dark panel joints. The wash is colour-changeable: photo W1 shows the band between column heads and soffit saturated **red-orange (about #FF4A1F)** while the outer fascia stays cream.
- **Columns:** lit from narrow up/down lights at the base and head; they read as pale vertical strokes against a darker wall.
- **Strip windows:** glow **cyan-blue (about #22A8FF)**, forming a diagonal dashed line across the leaning wall.

- **Entrance:** downlights in the soffit above the doors, white lit "K-Arena" letters, red interior behind the glass.
- **Roof sign:** lit white, readable from across the river.
- **Tents:** the Arena Tent's sawtooth gables are lit warm from inside, giving a row of small glowing triangles.
- **Podium:** white ribbed wall grazed by cool-white light with tree shadows; brick tower lit warm amber; garden uplights under shrubs and trees; black lamp posts with warm lanterns; warm LED lines under some stair treads.
- **Towers:** Hilton has warm-white vertical line lighting on its piers and a white crown line; K Tower has a white crown outline with small red corner accents.
- **Hilton pilotis:** the "LED tree pillars" — brick octagonal columns whose white fan-vault capitals carry thin white LED lines (T7).

### 7.5 Hilton Yokohama and K Tower look

- **Hilton tower:** slab about 62 x 33 m, 26F. Strong vertical stripes: off-white piers about 1.2 m wide alternating with recessed window strips of blue-grey glass with **champagne / bronze-coloured frames** and spandrels; about 14 window strips on the long face. Plain projecting cornice; rooftop plant behind a parapet; red "H" helipad circle on the roof (S1). Art-deco themed interiors.
- **Hilton podium (3 storeys, about 15 m):** red-brown brick piers on white stone bases at deck level, a white classical frame with large black-framed windows above, and a dark slate-grey **mansard roof** with small white dormers and floodlight brackets. Reads like a Yokohama red-brick warehouse.
- **BAYDECK pavilion:** granite-clad square piers, black steel-framed glazing, dark mansard roof, planted roof terrace; industrial "hammerhead crane and container" theme inside.
- **K Tower:** about 42 x 32 m, 21F. Same white vertical piers but with **darker blue-black glass and dark frames**, so it reads darker than the Hilton; double-height glazed base with brick-red and granite piers.

### 7.6 Where queues form

No official drawing of queue lanes was found (gap). What is established:
- Entry is at Level 3 through Main / North / South entrances, the entrance being assigned per ticket (REPORTED, Travel Watch). Blue crowd-control fences stand in front of the main doors (T6) and black barrier racks are stored along the north wall (B11). ESTIMATE: entry queues snake on the arena terrace under the eave and spill down the terracotta stair onto the plaza.
- Goods queues form at the Arena Tent on the north terrace (11 sales bays).
- After shows there is regulated exit (規制退場): gates near the bicycle parking are closed, non-route stairs are closed, and flows are split to the South Gate (Shin-Takashima / Minatomirai) and to the West Gate + deck (Yokohama Station). Reported waits of 30-60 min, peak 90 min, with bunching on the deck (REPORTED, from search-result summaries of k-arena.com exit notices and fan guides; the notice pages themselves returned 404 when opened).

## 10. Suggested parametric recipe for the modeller (all ESTIMATED, built from the facts above)

1. Extrude the hexagon P1..P7 as the roof outline. Roof top +45 m along the front facets falling to +36 m at the stage end; add the axial ridge (rise about 1.5 m over the long-side edges).
2. Front facets: offset the roof edge inward 9 m for the column line; place 1.2 m square columns from 0 to +36 m at about 8 m centres (10 / 8 / 8). Offset a further 9-10 m for the terrace-level wall. Build the leaning wall from (terrace wall line, +14 m) out to (1 m inside the column line, +35 m).
3. Eave slab: wedge from the wall top (+35 m) through the column heads (+36 m) to the lip (+41 m) with a 1.2 m radius nose; skirt back up to +45 m over 6 m.
4. Glass box: 46 m x 11 m x 20 m centred on the main facet on the terrace (+6 m); open bottom storey 4.5 m with 5-6 door bays.
5. Long sides: vertical walls on edges P1-P2 and P5-P7, parapet +43 m to +32 m; ribbed wedge at the fan end; recessed opening 10 x 13 m starting about 40 m from the stage-end corner and about 13 m above the podium band; louvre band; dark base.
6. Stage end: round the two corners (R 3 m); add a 20 m wide detached block with a 4 m recess.
7. Decks: terrace +6 m around the three front facets (width about 25-30 m beyond the wall), plaza +4.5 m per the podium polygon; terracotta stair between them; north grand stair to street.
8. Add towers from the OSM rectangles at 100 m, podiums at 15 m, sky-bridge, tents, deck, props.
